📖 How-To Guide
Everything you need to know about using the Rewards & Recognition Portal — for users and admins alike
📅
Submission Timelines & Deadlines
Deadlines for each award cycle — both Quarterly Awards and Star of the Month — are announced and shared with all team members well in advance before the cycle opens. You will receive a communication via email and/or team channels with the exact open date, submission window, and closing deadline for that period.

Please do not wait until the last day. Nominations submitted close to the deadline may not receive the attention they deserve. We encourage everyone to nominate as soon as they witness outstanding work worth celebrating.

📣 Watch out for the official announcement emails from the Natwest R&R Admin Team for upcoming cycle dates.

1 — What is This Portal?

The Accenture Natwest Rewards & Recognition (R&R) Portal is the official platform for celebrating exceptional work within the Natwest project team. It gives every team member an easy way to formally nominate colleagues who have gone above and beyond — and gives admins the tools to review, approve, and rank those nominations. It also provides a dedicated tracker for client appreciation messages received from clients and onshore stakeholders.

🏆
Quarterly Awards
Recognise standout individual or team performance over a full quarter. Six categories: Rising Star, Tech Star, Alchemist, Team Award, Exemplary Leader, and All Stars (admin-only).
⭐
Star of the Month
A monthly spotlight for someone who truly stood out — quick, impactful, and easy to submit. Great for recognising day-to-day excellence without waiting for the quarterly cycle.
✨
Client Appreciations
Log and track appreciation emails/messages received from clients and onshore stakeholders. Each entry requires a validator who was copied or forwarded on the original email.
📋
Transparent Tracking
All submissions are visible to registered users. Filter, sort, search by any column, and download Excel reports for both nominations and appreciations at any time.
🔐
2 — Logging In & Your Account
1 Enter your Accenture email

On the login screen, type your full @accenture.com email address and click Continue. Only pre-registered Accenture emails are accepted — if yours hasn't been registered yet, contact an Admin.

2 Enter your password

Your initial password is set by the Admin when they register your account. You can change it at any time using the "Change Password" link below the password field on the login screen — before signing in.

💡 First time logging in? Use the initial password provided by your Admin. You are strongly encouraged to change it immediately using the Change Password link. Passwords must be at least 8 characters. If you ever forget your password, contact an Admin who can reset it for you from the Admin panel.
✍️
3 — Submitting a Nomination
🏆 Quarterly Awards Nomination
1

Select a Category — Choose from the award category cards at the top: Rising Star, Tech Star, Alchemist, Team Award, Exemplary Leader. The All Stars category is visible to admins only. Click a card to select it — it highlights in purple.

2

Fill in the Nominee Details — Enter the nominee's full name (letters and spaces only, max 45 chars) and their @accenture.com email. For Team Awards, the primary fields cover the lead member — click "＋ Add Member" to add additional members, each needing a unique @accenture.com email.

3

Team Name & Sub Team — Select the team (AMS, Data Solutions, Shared Services, CIF, or Other) and optionally enter the sub-team (e.g. Data Engineering, EDL, Testing, Design, PMO, UXP). Do not enter a Squad Name in the sub-team field.

4

Add a Photograph (optional) — Click the upload zone or drag & drop a .jpg file under 1 MB. The photo is stored with the nomination, shown in the detail view, and indicated by a purple dot (●) in the table. Only one photo per nomination.

5

Write the Reason / Citation — Describe specifically what the person did, the impact it had, and the behaviour they demonstrated. Be concrete — up to 1,500 characters (live counter shown). A strong citation significantly increases the chance of approval.

6

Submit — Click "Submit Nomination →". A toast confirms success and the nomination immediately appears in All Submissions as Pending. The portal blocks re-nominating the same person in the same category within the same calendar month — re-nominations are allowed from the next month onward.

⭐ Star of the Month Nomination

Star of the Month follows the same process — name, email, team, sub-team, optional photo, and reason — but no category selection is required. Go to the ⭐ Star of the Month tab and fill in the form directly. The same monthly duplicate check applies.

✅ Tip: Star of the Month is ideal for recognising someone who made a difference right now — a great client interaction, clutch delivery, or quietly keeping things running. You don't need to wait for the quarterly cycle.
⚠ Important: Nominations are submitted under your logged-in identity. You can edit or delete your own Pending nominations from the All Submissions tab. Once approved, editing is no longer available.
📋
4 — Viewing All Submissions

The All Submissions tab is the central view of every nomination. All registered users can view this page.

🔍
Search
Instantly filter nominations by nominee name, email, team, nominated-by, or category using the search box.
🎛
Quick Filters
Pill buttons above the table: All, Quarterly only, Star/Month only, Pending only, or Approved only.
▼ ↕
Column Filters & Sort
Click ▼ on any column header for Excel-style multi-value filtering. Click ↕ to sort ascending/descending — a third click clears the sort.
⬇ ✔
Download Reports
⬇ Download Excel Report exports all nominations. ✔ Download Approved Nominations exports only approved entries — both as CSV files.
👁
View Details
Click any nominee's name (underlined in purple) to open the full detail view — citation, photo, team members, rank, and approval info.
✏️ 🗑
Edit / Delete
Edit or delete nominations you submitted, as long as they are still Pending. Edit and Delete buttons appear only on your own rows.
Status Guide — ⏳ Pending = awaiting admin review. ✔ Approved = reviewed and finalised — a Rank may be assigned (🥇 🥈 🥉 for the top three). A purple dot (●) next to a name means a photo is attached.
✨
5 — Client Appreciations

The ✨ Client Appreciations tab logs and tracks appreciation messages received from clients and onshore stakeholders — creating a permanent, searchable record of positive feedback, separate from the awards process.

📝 Submitting an Appreciation Entry
Appreciation To

The @accenture.com email of the team member who received the appreciation. No spaces allowed.

Appreciated By

The email of the client or onshore stakeholder who sent the appreciation. Must be @accenture.com or @rbos.co.uk.

Email Subject

The subject line of the appreciation email (max 150 chars). Used for duplicate detection — two entries with the same subject and same 'Appreciation To' will be blocked.

Appreciation Words

The key phrase or sentence actually written about the person in the appreciation email (max 1000 chars).

Description

Context about why the client or stakeholder gave the appreciation — what work, interaction, or achievement prompted it (max 1500 chars).

Validator

The @accenture.com email of a person who can verify the appreciation — typically someone who was copied or forwarded on the original email. This helps admins confirm authenticity before approving.

📊 Managing the Appreciations List

Below the entry form is the full appreciations list, with the same search, column filter, sort, edit, and delete capabilities as All Submissions. Two Excel download buttons appear at the top of the list:

⬇ Download All Appreciations
Exports every entry (Pending and Approved) as CSV — for full records or reporting.
✔ Download Approved Appreciations
Exports only approved entries — ideal for presentations or clean dashboards of validated appreciations.
💡 Approval & Passcodes: Appreciation entries start as Pending and must be approved by an admin using the Approval Passcode — the same passcode used for nominations. You can edit or delete your own Pending entries. Use the ✕ button (with Deletion Passcode) to remove any entry.

6 — Admin Guide
Visible to Admin-role users only
🔒 Admin Only

The Admin panel is only accessible to users registered with the Admin role. Non-admin users will see a locked screen when visiting the Admin tab.

☁ Data Sync

Use "🔄 Sync Nominations" to pull the latest nomination data from the backend, and "🔄 Refresh Users" to reload the user list. Always sync after onboarding new users or if the display seems stale.

👥 User Management

Manage who can access the portal. Only registered @accenture.com users can log in — no self-registration. Use the search box, or the ▼ filter and ↕ sort icons on the Email, Name, Role and Registered column headers, to find users.

A

Register a New User — Click "＋ Register New User", fill in the Accenture email, full name, initial password, and role (User or Admin). Share the password securely and advise them to change it on first login.

B

Remove a User — Click Remove to deactivate a user account (prevents further logins). Their past nominations and appreciations are unaffected. You cannot remove your own account.

C

Reset a User's Password — Click "🔑 Reset PW" next to any user to set a new password. Communicate it to them securely; they can change it again after logging in.

D

Download Users List — Click "⬇ Download Users List" to export all registered users (name, email, role, registration date) as a CSV file.

🏅 Approving & Rejecting — Nominations & Appreciations

Admins action nominations from All Submissions and appreciation entries from Client Appreciations. The same passcode-protected flow applies to both:

✔ Approve

Click Approve on a Pending entry. Enter the Approval Passcode. For nominations, you must also pick the Month (Star of the Month) or Quarter (Quarterly awards) and the Year the award is approved for — these default from the submission date — and may optionally assign a numeric rank (1–99; medals auto-display for ranks 1–3). Status changes to Approved and the approver's email is recorded.

✖ Reject

Click Reject on an Approved entry to revert it to Pending. Requires the Rejection Passcode. Use this if an approval was made in error. The approved Month/Quarter and Year are cleared, and you are asked for them again on the next approval. The rejector's email is recorded.

✕ Delete

The ✕ button on each row permanently deletes the entry using the Deletion Passcode. Irreversible — use only for duplicates, errors, or inappropriate content.

🔑 Passcode Note: All passcodes are case-sensitive. The same passcode set is shared across nominations and client appreciations. Contact the Portal Product Owner if you need a passcode.
🔐 Passcode Operations Summary
Approval Passcode
Approve nominations (with optional rank) and client appreciation entries
Rejection Passcode
Revert an approved nomination or appreciation back to Pending
Deletion Passcode
Permanently delete any nomination or appreciation entry
Flush Passcode
Danger Zone — wipes all nominations OR all appreciations. End-of-cycle only.
⚠ Danger Zone — Flush All Entries

The Admin panel has two Flush buttons: "🗑 Flush All Nominations" permanently deletes all Quarterly & Star of the Month submissions, and "🗑 Flush All Appreciations" permanently deletes all Client Appreciation entries. Both are irreversible, require the Flush Passcode, and should only be used at end-of-cycle once all results have been communicated externally.

7 — Frequently Asked Questions
Can I nominate myself? ＋

Yes. Self-nominations are allowed.

Can I nominate the same person more than once? ＋

Yes — but not for the same category in the same calendar month. The portal blocks duplicate nominations for the same person and category within the same month. You may re-nominate in a new month, or nominate them in a different category or award type in the same month.

Can I attach a photo to a nomination? ＋

Yes — both Quarterly and Star of the Month forms include an optional photo upload field. Only .jpg files under 1 MB are accepted. A purple dot (●) appears next to the nominee's name in the table to indicate a photo is attached, and the photo is shown in the detail view.

What is the All Stars category and why can't I see it? ＋

The All Stars award category is reserved for admin-role users only. It is hidden from regular users and cannot be submitted by them. This category is used to recognise all-round performers at the discretion of the admin team.

When will I know the results? ＋

Results are communicated by the Admin team after the submission window closes. You can track nomination status in the All Submissions tab in real time. Approved nominations show ✔ Approved and may have a rank assigned.

I can't log in — what do I do? ＋

Your email may not be registered yet, or your password is incorrect. Contact the Admin team to have your account created or your password reset. Only @accenture.com addresses are accepted.

When does the submission window open and close? ＋

Timelines are published in advance by the Admin team via email and team channels. There is no automatic portal lock — it is the nominator's responsibility to submit within the communicated window. Late entries may not be considered for the current cycle.

What makes a strong nomination? ＋

The best nominations are specific and impactful. Describe the exact situation, what the person did, how it benefited the team or client, and which behaviour they demonstrated. Avoid vague phrases like "always does a great job" — use a concrete example instead. You have up to 1,500 characters — use them well.

Can an Admin see my password? ＋

No — absolutely not. Passwords are never stored in plain text. At the moment you set or change your password, it is immediately converted into an encrypted hash using SHA-256 — a one-way cryptographic method. The original password cannot be read, reversed, or retrieved by anyone, including Admins. If you forget it, an Admin can reset it to a new one — but they cannot see or recover your old one.
